The rigging process mainly involves the following steps:
1. Select the first joint, set it as the center, and add the first bone.
2. Select the center of the connection point for the next joint, and extend the first bone to the second joint.
3. Continue in this manner until all bones are created.
4. Attach these bones to the parts created during modeling.

I think the easiest part is creating the bones for each body part. The most difficult part is attaching the parts to the bones.
